Emergency? Call, don't message.

  • Water leaking or a burst pipe
  • No heat in cold weather
  • Smell of gas (leave first, then call the gas company and 911)
  • Sewer backup, or no power to the home
  • A door or window that won't lock

Contact us

You're renting from local owners, not a call center. Text is usually fastest. For anything about your lease or balance, you can also message us in the portal.

Common questions

How do I set up autopay?

Log in to the tenant portal, add your bank account or card, and turn on automatic payments. Rent then pays itself on the due date each month.

I paid by Zelle or Venmo. Why doesn't the portal show it yet?

Those payments come to us outside the portal, so we post them by hand. Give us a day or two. If it still isn't showing, send us a text.

I haven't gotten my portal invite.

Text or email Sam or Jack and we'll resend it. Check your spam folder for an email from Innago too.

I'm thinking about renewing or moving out.

Reach out at least 60 days before your lease ends (or whatever notice your lease spells out) and we'll talk through options.
